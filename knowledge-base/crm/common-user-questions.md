## Email, calendar & communication
**Is my email syncing?**
Check that your inbox is connected under settings. [Steps.] Reconnect if it's disconnected.

**Why isn't an automated email sending?**
Common causes: automation is off, the contact is unsubscribed, a required field is empty, or the sending limit was reached. Escalate if it's not obvious.

**Emails going to spam.**
Usually a domain authentication issue (SPF, DKIM, DMARC). Escalate; don't guess.

## Automation & integrations
**My form submissions aren't showing up.**
Check the form connection, spam filters, and required fields. Escalate with the form URL.

**An integration stopped working.**
Often an expired connection or changed password. Reconnect it. If that fails, escalate.

**Can you build an automation for X?**
Probably. Describe the trigger and the action. Larger builds are quoted. [Policy.]

**How much is my CRM subscription?**
License costs are paid to the vendor. We don't see that billing. Our support fees are per your agreement.

**Can I upgrade or downgrade?**

## When to escalate
Per `../general/contact-escalation.md`:
- Data loss or suspected unauthorized access (urgent)
- Anything blocking the sales team from working
- Billing disputes or contract changes
- Requests for custom work or reports
- Issues after reasonable troubleshooting
- Anything you can't answer from this file

## Reminders for the assistant
- Never ask for CRM passwords or API keys.
- Don't guess platform-specific steps. If they aren't listed here, escalate.
- Keep steps short, numbered, and in plain language.
