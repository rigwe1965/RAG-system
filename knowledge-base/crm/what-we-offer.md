## What a CRM is
A CRM (Customer Relationship Management) system is one place to track leads, customers, conversations, and sales. It helps businesses follow up faster, stay organized, and see what's working.

## Connect your CRM to an AI agent
CRM integration is the most requested thing we build for AI agents. Full detail is in `../ai-agency/ai-agent-integrations.md`.

**What the agent can do in your CRM**
- Create and update contacts, leads, deals, and tickets
- Look up customer history, order status, and previous conversations
- Log full chat transcripts with intent tags
- Trigger workflows: email sequences, task creation, stage changes
- Qualify leads and book meetings straight into calendars

**Typical first workflows**
- Website lead capture into the CRM, with the full conversation attached
- Support questions that need account data, such as "Where is my refund?"
- Order status and re-order suggestions (especially useful for the hair store)

**Typical timelines** (estimates)
- Basic read plus lead write: 3–10 days
- Full two-way with actions: 2–4 weeks

**How it stays safe:** least-privilege access, human approval for high-risk actions, full audit logs, and verified user identity before any account lookup. We recommend starting with one high-value workflow and expanding once it's proven.

## Common questions
**Which CRM is best?**
It depends on size, budget, industry, and needs. Ask a few questions (team size, current tools, budget, what they want to track), then recommend one of the platforms above or offer a consultation.

**Can you migrate my data from spreadsheets or another CRM?**
Yes. We clean and map the data, import it, and check for duplicates and errors, keeping a backup of the originals. Some history, such as email threads and attachments, may not transfer fully between systems. See `implementation.md`.

**How much will it cost?**
Give the range (net, plus VAT), explain price drivers, and ask about users and needs.

**Do I need a CRM?**
If they're tracking leads or customers in spreadsheets or email and missing follow-ups, probably yes.

**Can it connect to my website or chatbot?**
Usually. Ask which tools. An AI agent can also work inside your CRM, so leads, history, and tickets update automatically. Stress security, guardrails, human approval for risky actions, and starting with one workflow.
