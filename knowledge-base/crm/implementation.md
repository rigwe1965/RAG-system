# CRM: Implementation

How we set up a CRM, from first call to a team using it every day.

> Timelines are estimates, never guarantees. Say "typically".

## Process

### 1. Discovery & requirements
- How leads and customers are tracked today
- Sales stages, team roles, reports needed
- Tools to connect (website, email, calendar, accounting, e-commerce)
- Goals and success measures
- **Time:** typically 1–2 sessions

### 2. Platform & plan
- Confirm the platform (or recommend one)
- Design the pipeline, fields, users, permissions, and automations
- Written plan for approval
- **Time:** typically 3–5 days

### 3. Setup & configuration
- Create account structure, users, and roles
- Build pipelines, custom fields, and views
- Set up forms, email templates, and automations
- **Time:** typically 1–3 weeks

### 4. Data migration
- Clean and map data from spreadsheets or the old CRM
- Import contacts, companies, deals, and notes
- Check for duplicates and errors
- **Time:** depends on data volume and quality; estimated in the plan

### 5. Integrations
- Website forms, email and calendar sync, phone/SMS, e-commerce, chatbots, accounting
- **Time:** typically 1–3 days per integration

### 6. Testing
- Test automations, forms, permissions, and reports
- Review with the client and fix issues

### 7. Training & launch
- Staff training (live + recordings/guides). See below.
- Go live, often with a short parallel period
- **Time:** typically about 1 week

### 8. Support & optimization
- Check-ins after launch
- Adjust workflows based on real use
- Ongoing support plan (optional, monthly)

## Typical timelines
| Project | Estimated duration |
|---|---|
| Basic setup (small team, simple pipeline) | 1–2 weeks |
| Standard (migration, automations, integrations) | 3–6 weeks |
| Advanced / multi-team / custom | Scoped per project |

## What we need from the client
- A project owner who can make decisions
- Admin access to the CRM and connected tools
- Data exports (spreadsheets, old CRM exports)
- Sales process notes and key reports
- Branding and email templates
- Availability for sessions and testing

## Data migration notes
- Data quality matters: duplicates and missing fields should be cleaned up first.
- Some history (e.g. email threads, attachments) may not transfer fully. It depends on the platform; the migration plan says what will transfer.
- We keep a backup of original files.
- Basic cleanup (removing obvious duplicates) is part of the migration. Larger cleanups are quoted separately.

## Training
- **Format:** live sessions, recordings, and written guides
- **Who:** sales, support, management, admins
- **Length:** typically 1–2 hours per group
- **Follow-up:** a Q&A session about 1 week after launch

## Adoption: what makes it work
- Leadership uses and expects the CRM
- Keep the setup simple at first
- Required fields only where needed
- Regular reviews and quick fixes
- One clear owner

## Risks and how we manage them
| Risk | How we handle it |
|---|---|
| Staff don't use it | Training, simple setup, leadership buy-in |
| Dirty data | Cleanup before migration |
| Scope creep | Written scope, change requests quoted |
| Delays in client data | Clear checklist and deadlines |

## Common questions
**How long does it take?**
Give the estimate for their project type, noting dependence on data and feedback.

**Will we lose data?**
No. We back up originals and check imports. Some formatting or history may differ between systems.

**Will my team actually use it?**
Training and a simple setup help. We also offer follow-up support.

**Can we start small and add later?**
Yes. We recommend starting with the core pipeline and adding automation in phases.

**Do we have to stop working during setup?**
No. Setup happens alongside normal work; there's a short switchover period.
