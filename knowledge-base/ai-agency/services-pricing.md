- **Contract:** month-to-month after launch, with 30 days' notice to cancel.
- **Discounts and bundles:** none. Pricing is per service. The assistant must not offer or promise discounts.
- **Quotes:** all ranges are a guide. A final quote follows discovery.

## What affects the price
- Number of channels (web, WhatsApp, voice, etc.)
- Amount and quality of content to train on
- Integrations (CRM, RMM/PSA, calendar, e-commerce, helpdesk) and how many systems the agent connects to
- Whether actions need human approval, and the security setup required
- Conversation volume
- Languages
- Need for custom features or approvals

## What's included
Discovery, build, testing, launch, and a handover so your team knows how to use and review it. A post-launch support period is agreed in the proposal. Ongoing monitoring and improvements are available on a monthly plan.

## What's not included
- Writing your content (FAQs, policies, product information); we can help draft it, which may be quoted separately
- Third-party costs such as AI model usage, phone numbers, and software subscriptions (billed at cost)
- Major changes after the scope is signed off, which are quoted separately

## Questions to expect
**How much does a chatbot cost?**
Give the range (€650–€2,500 setup plus €40–€210 per month, plus VAT), then explain what drives cost and ask about their use case.

**Will it replace my staff?**
No. It handles routine questions so your team can focus on higher-value work, and it hands off to a person when needed.

**Is my data safe?**
We handle client data carefully: the AI only gets the minimum access it needs, one customer's data is kept separate from another's, risky actions need human approval, and every action is logged. See `ai-agent-integrations.md` for the safeguards we apply. For details on where data is stored, how long it is kept, and how it is used, say these are covered in the agreement and offer to pass the question to the team. Do not claim any certification, and do not say data is or isn't used to train AI models.

**Which AI models do you use?**
We choose the model based on the project. We commonly use Claude and other leading models, together with speech tools for voice agents.

**Can it connect to the tools I use?**
Usually. Ask which tools. We commonly integrate CRMs (HubSpot, Salesforce, Zoho, Pipedrive, Dynamics 365), RMM/PSA tools (NinjaOne, ConnectWise, Datto, Autotask, HaloPSA, Syncro), helpdesks and messaging channels, calendars, email, and e-commerce backends. For anything about connecting agents to systems, use `ai-agent-integrations.md`, and always stress security, guardrails, human approval for high-risk actions, and starting with one high-value workflow.

**What's the difference between a chatbot and an agent?**
A chatbot answers questions. An agent can also look things up and take approved actions in your systems. Most clients start with a chatbot and grow into an agent.

**Where should we start?**
With one high-value workflow, such as lead capture into the CRM or order status, then expand once it's proven.

**Can you guarantee results?**
We don't guarantee specific outcomes. We set clear goals, measure results, and improve over time.

**Do you offer discounts?**
No. Pricing is per service.
