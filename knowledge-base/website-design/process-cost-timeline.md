# Website Design: Process, Cost & Timeline

> Owner-approved pricing and timelines (November 2026). All prices are in euros and **net: 19% German VAT is added on top**. They are typical ranges, not fixed quotes. Anything not listed here goes to a human for a custom quote.

## What we do
We design and build websites that look professional, work on every device, and help the business get customers. Services include new sites, redesigns, e-commerce stores, landing pages, and ongoing care.

## Packages & pricing
| Package | Best for | Typical scope | Price range (plus VAT) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Starter / landing page | New or small businesses | 1–3 pages, contact form | €400–€1,250 |
| Business website | Established businesses | 5–10 pages, SEO basics, blog or gallery | €1,700–€5,000 |
| E-commerce store | Selling products online | Product pages, cart, checkout, payments | €3,000–€8,500 |

### What changes the price
- Number of pages
- Custom design vs. a template-based design
- E-commerce size (number of products, shipping and tax rules)
- Integrations (CRM, booking, payments, email marketing)
- Content: who writes the copy and provides photos
- Special features (multilingual, member areas, custom forms)
- Timeline (rush work may cost more)

### Not included unless stated in the proposal
Domain registration, hosting, premium plugins or themes, stock photos, copywriting, and paid advertising. Copywriting is available as an extra and is quoted separately.

### Payment terms
50% deposit to start, 25% at design approval, 25% before launch. Ongoing care plans are billed monthly; see `maintenance.md`. Prices are a guide, and a final quote follows discovery.

## Process

### 1. Discovery
Goals, audience, competitors, features, content, and budget. Usually a call plus a short questionnaire at the start of the project.

### 2. Proposal & agreement
Scope, price, timeline, and terms. The deposit starts the project.

### 3. Design
Layout and look based on your brand: a homepage mockup first, then the inner pages.
- **Revisions:** 2 rounds of design revisions are included. Extra rounds are billed hourly.
- **Time:** typically 1–2 weeks for a business website.

### 4. Build
Development, content loading, and integrations. Responsive on phone, tablet, and desktop.
- **Time:** typically 2–4 weeks for a business website.

### 5. Review & testing
You review. We test links, forms, speed, devices, and browsers.
- **Time:** typically 3–7 days.

### 6. Launch
Final approval, domain and hosting setup, go live.

### 7. After launch
Support and maintenance. See `maintenance.md`.

## Common questions
**How much does a website cost?**
Give the relevant range (net, plus VAT), explain what drives cost, then ask about pages, features, and timing.

**How long will it take?**
Give the estimate for their project type, noting it depends on content and feedback.

**Do you write the content?**
You provide the text and photos. We can write the copy as an extra, quoted separately.

**Will it work on mobile?**
Yes. All sites are responsive.

**Will I be able to edit it myself?**
Usually yes. We build on platforms you can edit and show you how at handover. The platform depends on your needs; see `tech-stack.md`.

**Will it show up on Google?**
We include SEO basics: page titles and descriptions, a clean page structure, and a sitemap. Rankings depend on many factors and can't be guaranteed.

**Who owns the site?**
You own the design, code, and content once the final payment is made. Third-party licences and platform terms (for example a store platform's subscription) still apply.

**Do you offer discounts?**
Don't promise any. Pricing is quoted per project; escalate if the customer asks for one.
