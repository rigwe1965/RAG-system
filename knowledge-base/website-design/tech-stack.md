## Platforms we build on
| Platform | Best for | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| WordPress | Business sites, blogs, flexible needs | Easy to edit; huge plugin ecosystem; needs updates and security care |
| Shopify | E-commerce | Fast to launch; payments and inventory built in; monthly fee |
| Webflow | Design-heavy marketing sites | Polished visuals; hosted; clean editing |
| Custom code (React / Next.js) | Complex or custom web apps | Most flexible; higher cost |


## How we choose
We recommend the simplest platform that meets the goals and budget. Factors:
- Does the client want to edit the site themselves?
- Is it a store, a booking site, or a marketing site?
- Budget and timeline
- Need for custom features or integrations
- Long-term maintenance
