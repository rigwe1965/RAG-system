# Hair Store: Sizing & Quantity

Helps the assistant answer "how many bundles do I need?" and "what size should I get?". The numbers below are common industry guidelines. Adjust them to match your actual bundle weights and products, and always present them as estimates.

> Items in [BRACKETS] are for the owner to fill in.

## Key facts
- **Bundle weight:** each bundle weighs 100 g. The quantities below are based on this. They are estimates, so don't promise an exact number; a stylist may adjust.
- **Length:** measured from root to tip with the hair stretched straight. Curly and wavy textures look shorter once worn, since the pattern pulls the hair up.
- **Fuller look = more bundles or a shorter length.** Longer lengths need more hair to look full.

## How many bundles?
General guideline for a sew-in (adjust to the exact product):

| Length | Bundles for a standard look | For a fuller look |
|---|---|---|
| 10"–14" | 2–3 | 3 |
| 16"–20" | 3 | 3–4 |
| 22"–26" | 3–4 | 4 |
| 28"+ | 4 | 4–5 |

- **With a closure or frontal:** usually 2–3 bundles plus the piece, depending on length and fullness.
- **Curly textures:** may need an extra bundle for the same fullness.
- **A wig** needs no extra bundles.

## Mixing lengths
Mixing lengths (e.g. 16" 18" 20") gives a layered, natural look. Matching lengths gives a blunt, full look. Ask which the customer prefers.

## Closure and frontal sizes
| Piece | Typical size | Coverage |
|---|---|---|
| Closure | 4x4, 5x5, 6x6 | Center / part area |
| Frontal | 13x4, 13x6 | Ear to ear hairline |

- **Match the piece to the bundles:** same texture, origin, and length. A closure or frontal is often one length shorter or the same as the bundles. [Confirm for our products.]
- **Lace color:** choose the shade closest to the customer's skin tone. We offer HD lace, transparent lace, and full (360) lace; HD lace blends into most skin tones.

## Wig sizing
- **Cap size:** our wigs have one adjustable size that fits most heads, typically head circumferences of about 21–23 inches (53–58 cm). Check the product page, and don't promise a fit.
- **How to measure:** use a soft measuring tape around the head, starting at the front hairline, going behind the ears and along the nape of the neck, and back to the start. Keep it snug but not tight.
- **Adjustments:** adjustable strap or elastic band at the back. [Combs: confirm per product.]
- **Density:** 150% = natural, 180% = full, 200% = extra full, 250% = very full. Options vary by wig.

## What to ask before recommending
1. Product: bundles, closure, frontal, or wig?
2. Desired length and texture?
3. How full do they want it?
4. Their height, or where they want it to fall?
5. Budget?

Then give a recommendation, e.g.: "For a full 20-inch body wave sew-in with a closure, I'd suggest 3 bundles plus a 5x5 closure. Want me to pull up options in that range?"

## Reminders for the assistant
- Present quantities as **estimates**; say stylists may adjust.
- If the customer is unsure, suggest the higher quantity rather than risk running short.
- Don't promise exact looks. If a product isn't covered here, escalate.
