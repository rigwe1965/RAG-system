# Hair Store: Ordering, Shipping & Returns

> Owner-approved policy (November 2026), based on the owner's choices: worldwide shipping, €4.90 within Germany (free over €75), 14-day returns, payment by card, PayPal, Klarna, and bank transfer.
> Items in [BRACKETS] are still unknown. The assistant must not state a bracketed value; escalate instead.
> All hair store prices are in euros and include 19% VAT. This policy follows German consumer rules for online sales to consumers; have a lawyer check the exact wording before it goes on the website.

## How to order
- **Where:** in our online shop. Orders and questions can also be sent by email or WhatsApp.
- **Steps:** choose product, length, and texture; add to cart; check out.
- **Payment methods:** credit/debit card, PayPal, Klarna (buy now, pay later), and bank transfer (Vorkasse).
- **Bank transfer:** the order is reserved until payment arrives and ships once we receive it. Payment is due within 7 days, otherwise the order is cancelled.
- **Klarna:** payment terms are set by Klarna at checkout.
- **Order confirmation:** an email is sent right after checkout. Check the spam folder if it doesn't arrive.
- **Changing or cancelling an order:** contact us as soon as possible by email or WhatsApp. We can change or cancel an order only if it hasn't shipped yet. After it ships, use the returns process.

## Processing time
- Orders ship within **1–3 business days** after payment is received.
- Orders placed after 14:00 (Germany time), or on weekends and holidays, ship the next business day.
- Custom or made-to-order items (for example, custom wigs) take **7–14 business days** to make before shipping.

## Order status and problems
- **Where is my order?** Check the tracking email. If it's past the estimated window, escalate with the order number.
- **Wrong item, damaged, or missing item:** contact us as soon as you notice it, with the order number and photos, even if the item was delivered some days ago. Escalate per `../general/contact-escalation.md`. Your statutory warranty rights for defective goods are not affected by this policy.
- **Wrong address:** we can correct it only **before the order ships**. After shipping, we can't change it, and we aren't responsible for an order sent to an address the customer entered incorrectly.
- **Lost package:** if tracking shows no movement for **7 business days**, contact us and we'll open a claim with the carrier.

## Returns & right of withdrawal
- **Withdrawal period:** **14 days** from the day you receive the goods. Tell us clearly that you want to return the order (email, WhatsApp, or contact form); sending the goods back alone is not enough.
- **Condition:** the hair must be returned **unused and in resalable condition**, in its original packaging. You may look at it and try it on briefly, as you would in a shop. It must not be installed, washed, colored, bleached, cut, plucked, or otherwise altered. If it is returned altered or worn beyond a brief try-on, we may reduce the refund by the loss in value or refuse the return.
- **Not returnable:**
  - Made-to-measure or customized items (for example, custom wigs). These are exempt from the right of withdrawal.
  - Hair that has been installed, washed, colored, cut, or otherwise altered.
  - Items damaged by the customer.
- **How to return:** contact us first at info@faiyke.com or via WhatsApp +49 176 41565516 with the order number. We send return instructions. Don't ship anything back before we reply.
- **Return shipping:** paid by the customer, unless the return is due to our error (wrong or defective item).
- **Refunds:** we refund the purchase price, **including the original standard shipping cost**, to the original payment method within **14 days** of receiving your withdrawal notice. We may wait until we have received the goods back or you have shown proof of shipment. Extra costs for a more expensive delivery option you chose are not refunded.
- **Exchanges:** yes, for a different length or texture, subject to availability, on unused items within the 14-day period.
- **Store credit:** not offered.
