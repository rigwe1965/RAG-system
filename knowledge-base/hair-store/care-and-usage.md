# Hair Store: Care & Usage

> General care guidance for human hair. It is not a guarantee of results. For anything specific to one product, check its product page or ask the team.

## Before wearing
- Check the hair when it arrives and before installing, coloring, or cutting. Hair that has been installed, washed, colored, cut, or otherwise altered can't be returned (see `ordering-shipping-returns.md`).
- Gently detangle. If you like, wash and condition the hair before the install.
- Wigs: you can customize the lace, hairline, and fit before wearing, but customized wigs can't be returned. Whether a wig is pre-plucked or has bleached knots depends on the product; check the product page and don't guess.

## Washing
- Wash when needed, usually every 1–2 weeks of regular wear, with a sulfate-free shampoo.
- Use lukewarm water. Work down the hair; don't scrub, twist, or rub.
- Condition mid-length to ends, not the roots or wefts. Rinse well.
- Blot with a towel. Don't wring.

## Drying and detangling
- Air dry when possible, or use a blow dryer on low heat.
- Detangle from the ends upward with a wide-tooth comb or paddle brush.
- Be extra gentle with wet hair, which is more fragile.

## Heat styling
- Use a heat protectant every time.
- Keep tools at or below about 180 °C (350 °F), and lower for fine or colored hair.
- Don't hold a hot tool on one section for long.
- Heat is cumulative: frequent high heat will wear the hair out faster.

## Coloring
- Human hair can generally be colored, but results vary with the hair and any earlier processing, so we can't promise a specific outcome.
- Going darker is safer than going lighter. Lightening can weaken the hair and isn't recommended on hair that has already been lightened or heavily processed.
- Use a professional stylist, do a strand test first, and use a color-safe routine afterwards.
- Colored or lightened hair can't be returned (see `ordering-shipping-returns.md`).

## Daily and night care
- Detangle gently each day and wear loose protective styles.
- Sleep with a satin bonnet or scarf, or on a satin pillowcase.
- Avoid heavy oils or products on the lace and near the roots and wefts.

## Storage
- Store wigs on a wig stand or head form, or in a breathable bag.
- Keep hair dry and away from heat and direct sunlight.

## Installation notes
- **Sew-ins:** have a trained stylist install them. Maintenance is commonly needed every 6–8 weeks, but ask your stylist.
- **Closures and frontals:** methods include glue-less, adhesive, or tape. If you use adhesive or tape, do a patch test first, and follow the product's instructions. Stop and seek advice if your skin reacts.
- **Wigs:** check cap size, adjustable straps, combs, and bands on the product page. Measure your head before choosing a size (see `sizing-quantity.md`).

## How long it lasts
It depends on the texture, how often it's worn, and how it's cared for. With gentle washing, detangling, night protection, and limited heat, it stays in better condition for longer. We don't give a fixed number of months.

## Common problems
| Problem | Likely cause | What to suggest |
|---|---|---|
| Tangling | Dry ends, friction, no detangling | Gentle detangling from the ends, deep conditioner, satin bonnet |
| Shedding | Rough handling, worn wefts, product buildup | Handle gently; escalate if a new unit sheds heavily |
| Frizz | Dryness, heat, humidity | Moisturize, reduce heat, use a leave-in product |
| Odor or buildup | Infrequent washing or product residue | Wash and condition; occasionally use a clarifying shampoo |
| Lace tearing | Rough plucking or adhesive removal | Handle gently; use a remover made for lace and adhesives |

Anything that sounds like a defect (heavy shedding in a new unit, damaged wefts) should be escalated per `../general/contact-escalation.md`, with the order number and photos.

## Reminders for the assistant
- Don't give medical or scalp-health advice. For irritation, an allergic reaction, or hair-loss concerns, recommend seeing a professional.
- Don't promise results. Use "typically" and "with proper care".
