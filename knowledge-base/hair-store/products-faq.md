## Lace and wig specs
- **Lace types:** HD lace, transparent lace, and full (360) lace. Glueless, pre-cut lace wigs are also available. HD lace is the thinnest and blends into most skin tones; transparent lace is slightly thicker. Not every lace type is available on every product; check the product page.
- **Closure sizes:** 4x4, 5x5, and 6x6.
- **Frontal sizes:** 13x4 (ear to ear, standard hairline) and 13x6 (deeper parting space).
- **Wig density:** 150%, 180%, 200%, and 250%. 150% is a natural, lighter look; 180% is full but natural; 200% is extra full; 250% is very full. Density options vary by wig; check the product page.
- **Wig cap size:** one adjustable size that fits most heads, with an adjustable strap or elastic band. If a customer isn't sure it will fit, ask them to measure their head circumference (see `sizing-quantity.md`) and offer to check with the team. Don't promise a fit.

## Common questions

**Is the hair real human hair?**
Yes. Our hair is 100% human hair. We don't make further claims about it being virgin, raw, or remy, or about where it comes from. If a customer wants more detail, offer to pass the question to the team.

**Can I color, bleach, or heat style it?**
Human hair can generally be styled with heat and colored, but results depend on the hair and how it has been processed before, so we can't promise a specific result. Going darker is safer than going lighter, and lightening can weaken the hair. We recommend a professional stylist, a strand test first, and a heat protectant with tools at a moderate temperature. Colors and heat can affect how the hair wears over time. For care details, see `care-and-usage.md`. Don't promise results; offer to check with the team for a specific product.

**How long does the hair last?**
It depends on the texture, how often it's worn, and how it's cared for. With gentle washing, detangling, protection at night, and limited heat, it stays in better condition for longer. We don't give a fixed number of months. See `care-and-usage.md` for care tips.

**Does it shed or tangle?**
Some shedding and a little tangling are normal with any human hair, especially at the ends and when it's new. Gentle detangling from the ends up, regular conditioning, and a satin bonnet or pillowcase at night keep it in good shape (see `care-and-usage.md`). If a new unit sheds heavily or the wefts are coming apart, ask for the order number and photos and escalate to the team.

**Will the lace match my skin tone?**
Choose the lace shade closest to your skin tone. HD and transparent lace blend in most easily. Lace can also be tinted or touched up with a bit of makeup for a closer match. If the customer is unsure which shade to pick, ask them to describe their skin tone and offer to check with the team. Don't promise a perfect match.

**Are wigs pre-plucked / bleached knots?**
This depends on the individual wig. Check the product page for the details of that wig. If the customer asks about a specific wig and the page doesn't say, don't guess. Offer to check with the team. A wig can also be customized (plucked, bleached, cut) after delivery, but note that customized or altered wigs can't be returned (see `ordering-shipping-returns.md`).

**Can I see photos or videos of the actual hair?**
Product photos are on the product pages in the shop. If the customer wants photos or a video of a specific item, offer to ask the team and take their contact details for a hand-off. Don't promise that they will be sent.

**Do you offer installation or styling services?**
We sell the hair. We don't have confirmed information about installation or styling services, so don't say that we offer them or that we don't. We recommend a trained stylist for sew-ins, closures and frontals. If the customer asks about installation or a local stylist, offer to pass the question to the team.

**Do you offer wholesale or bulk pricing?**
Bulk and wholesale requests are handled by the team case by case. Don't quote a discount or a minimum, and don't promise one. Ask what they want to buy and how often, take their name and contact details, and hand them over to the team (follow `../general/lead-qualification.md`).
