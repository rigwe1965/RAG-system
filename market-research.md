## What this suggests for Faiyke
- **Textures:** the common set is straight, body wave, loose wave, deep wave, water wave, curly, kinky curly, kinky straight, yaki. I used it as a proposal in `products-faq.md`.
- **Lace:** HD and transparent; closures 4x4/5x5/6x6; frontals 13x4/13x6. Same for wigs, with 180% and 200% densities.
- **Prices:** US bundles run about $33–60 each, 3-bundle deals $139–160, wigs $72–560. My euro prices include 19% VAT and sit somewhat above the cheapest US shops because shipping and customs for EU buyers cost more. They are a starting guess, not your costs.
- **Competitive terms:** most US stores offer 30-day returns and free shipping over about $100. Faiyke's 14-day returns are the legal minimum in Germany; 30 days would be an easy way to stand out.
- **Trust:** the stronger stores say clearly whether hair is virgin, raw or remy and where it comes from. Only claim what your supplier can document.
- **German buyers** value invoice payment (Kauf auf Rechnung) and clear delivery times. Klarna covers part of that.
- Only Global Extend sells to Germany from Germany; the US stores ship from the US or China, so delivery speed and customs are a possible advantage for a Dortmund-based shop.
